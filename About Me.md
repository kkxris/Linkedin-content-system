# AI / Founder Transition

The move into AI was an extension of my marketing career, not a completely separate path.

After years of working in social media and content, I kept seeing the same problems: too many tools, approval loops, fragmented workflows, and teams expected to handle strategy, writing, visuals, reporting, and trends at once.

That frustration became the starting point for SocialFlo.

I went from being a marketer frustrated by the problem to a **non-technical founder building a solution for it**. I taught myself by building with AI and Claude Code, experimenting, breaking things, debugging, and learning what marketers actually need to understand versus what should be left to technical experts.

This is also what shaped my AI education. I don't teach AI as a collection of prompts or tools. I focus on **context, systems, workflows, and actually building things**.

## SocialFlo — Expanded Context

SocialFlo is an AI-powered social content platform for founders, B2B brands, startups, marketing teams, and personal brands.

It is built around the idea that people don't usually lack ideas. They lack a system for turning those ideas into consistent, strategic, on-brand content.

The product brings together strategy, ideas, copy, visuals, and platform-ready content in one workflow.

Key product thinking:

- Strategy before generation
- AI that understands brand context and voice
- Weeks or a month of content from a structured system
- One idea adapted across platforms
- Text and visuals together
- Less tool switching and fewer revision loops
- Consistency without generic AI content or burnout

I am building and testing it closely with early users and using the product myself.

A key part of the story is that I built it from a marketer's perspective rather than coming from a traditional software engineering background.

## Co-Work & 	

Co-Work & is a Berlin community for ambitious people who want to **learn, get work done, and build meaningful connections**.

The idea came from finding it difficult to build meaningful connections in Berlin.

I believe traditional networking often creates contacts, not relationships. Co-Work & creates connection through shared work, learning, workshops, collaboration, and experiences.

The community hosts **online and in-person events in Berlin**, including focused co-working sessions and hands-on AI workshops.

The format is intentionally more than a standard co-working meetup:

- Focused productivity sprints so people actually get work done
- Intentional networking and conversations during breaks
- Lunch and shared experiences
- Activities beyond work, from sports to after-work experiences
- Workshops where people learn by doing
